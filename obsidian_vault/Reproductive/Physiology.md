## Delayed puberty


Absence of 2° sexual characteristics by age 13 years in females and 14 years in males. Causes:

- Hypergonadotropic (1°) hypogonadism: Klinefelter syndrome, Turner syndrome, gonadal injury (eg, chemotherapy, radiotherapy, infection).

- Hypogonadotropic (2°) hypogonadism: constitutional delay of growth and puberty (“late blooming”), Kallmann syndrome, CNS lesions.
