**Persistent pulmonary hypertension of the newborn**

Persistence of � pulmonary vascular resistance after birth. Associated with abnormal development and postpartum adaptation of pulmonary vasculature. Risk factors include aspiration of meconiumstained amniotic fluid and neonatal pneumonia. Leads to right-to-left shunt through foramen ovale and ductus arteriosus. Preductal O2 saturation is often > postductal. Newborn presents with signs of [[Respiratory|respiratory]] distress (eg, tachypnea) and (often differential) cyanosis. Equal pulses (no delay).
