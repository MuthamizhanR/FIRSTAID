## [[Endocrine|Endocrine]]


` [[Embryology|Embryology]] 330 ` [[Anatomy|Anatomy]] 331 ` [[Physiology|Physiology]] 332 ` [[Pathology|Pathology]] 342 ` Pharmacology 358

The [[Endocrine|endocrine]] system comprises widely distributed organs that work in a highly integrated manner to orchestrate a state of hormonal equilibrium within the body. Generally speaking, [[Endocrine|endocrine]] diseases can be classified either as diseases of underproduction or overproduction, or as conditions involving the development of mass lesions—which themselves may be associated with underproduction or overproduction of hormones. Therefore, study the [[Endocrine|endocrine]] system first by learning the glands, their hormones, and their regulation, and then by integrating disease manifestations with diagnosis and management. Take time to learn the multisystem connections.
