**Neoplasia and** Uncontrolled, often monoclonal proliferation of cells. Can be benign or malignant. Any neoplastic **neoplastic progression** growth has two components: parenchyma (neoplastic cells) and supporting stroma (nonneoplastic; eg, blood vessels, connective tissue).
