**Clearance (CL)**

The volume of plasma cleared of drug per unit time. Clearance may be impaired with defects in cardiac, hepatic, or [[Renal|renal]] function.

**Half-life (t1/2)**

The time required to eliminate 1/2 of the drug from the body. Steady state is a dynamic equilibrium in which drug concentration stays constant (ie, rate of drug elimination = rate of drug administration).

In first-order kinetics, a drug infused at a constant rate takes 4–5 half-lives to reach steady state. It takes 3.3 half-lives to reach 90% of the steady-state level. # of half-lives 1 2 3 4 t1/2 =<sup>0.7 × Vd</sup> in first-order elimination CL
